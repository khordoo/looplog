## Maintenance process

1. Before each public release, open every canonical YouTube URL and confirm the title, current playable video, and visible movement/setup still match the catalog text.
2. For band items, specifically confirm a continuous 41-inch-style loop, no door anchor, no furniture anchor, and no handles unless the written setup is revised in the same content change.
3. Record the date, source, video ID, and fit decision in this table. Keep exactly one source per exercise; do not substitute a search-results page or playlist.
4. If a video is removed, altered, blocked, or mismatched, set its fit to `needs-replacement`, stop exposing it as production media, and replace it with a newly verified direct video ID.
5. The app must remain usable without media: render the written guide offline, report that video playback needs an internet connection, and offer the canonical Open YouTube link.
6. Recheck at least quarterly and whenever a creator changes a title, thumbnail, description, or upload. Re-run the content media-ID and attribution tests after edits.
